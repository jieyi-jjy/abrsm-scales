各调之间的规律 How the keys relate

顺时针，多一个升号

每往上一个纯五度（C→G→D…），多一个升号。新加的升号总是新调的第 7 级音：G 大调的 F♯，D 大调的 C♯。

Each step up a perfect fifth adds one sharp. The new sharp is always the 7th degree of the new key: F♯ in G major, C♯ in D major.

逆时针，多一个降号

每往下一个纯五度（C→F→B♭…），多一个降号。新加的降号总是新调的第 4 级音：F 大调的 B♭，B♭ 大调的 E♭。

Each step down a fifth adds one flat. The new flat is always the 4th degree of the new key: B♭ in F major, E♭ in B♭ major.

升降号的固定顺序

升号永远按 F C G D A E B 出现，降号正好倒过来：B E A D G C F。有 3 个升号，就一定是 F♯ C♯ G♯。

Sharps always arrive as F C G D A E B; flats in the reverse order, B E A D G C F. Three sharps are always F♯ C♯ G♯.

关系大小调共用调号

大调主音往下数小三度（3 个半音）就是它的关系小调：C 大调 ↔ A 小调。两者调号完全相同，在五度圈上内外相对。

A minor third (3 semitones) below a major tonic is its relative minor: C major ↔ A minor. They share one key signature and sit opposite each other on the two rings.

三种小调

自然小调只用调号。和声小调把第 7 级升高半音。旋律小调上行升高第 6、7 级，下行还原成自然小调。

Natural minor uses only the key signature. Harmonic minor raises the 7th. Melodic minor raises the 6th and 7th going up and returns to natural minor coming down.

指法的骨架：3 + 4

每个八度 7 个音，分成一组 3 个（1-2-3）和一组 4 个（1-2-3-4）。拇指只弹白键；4 指每个八度只用一次，记住它落在哪个音，整条音阶的指法就定了。

Seven notes per octave split into a group of three (1-2-3) and a group of four (1-2-3-4). The thumb stays on white keys. Finger 4 plays once per octave, so knowing its note fixes the whole fingering.

降号大调的口诀

F、B♭、E♭、A♭、D♭ 大调：右手 4 指永远落在 B♭。B♭、E♭、A♭、D♭ 大调：左手 4 指落在第 4 级音（也就是最新加的那个降号）。

In F, B♭, E♭, A♭ and D♭ major the right-hand 4th finger always lands on B♭. In B♭, E♭, A♭ and D♭ major the left-hand 4th finger lands on the 4th degree, the newest flat.

反向音阶

两手从同一个主音出发，右手向上、左手向下。每只手的指法和平时完全一样：右手用上行指法，左手用下行指法。C、G、D、A、E 大调里两手恰好镜像，每一步都用同一根手指。

Both hands start on the same tonic; the right goes up, the left goes down. Each hand keeps its usual fingering. In C, G, D, A and E major the hands mirror each other, using the same finger at every step.

三度相距与六度相距

两手弹同一条音阶，只是起点不同。三度相距：左手在主音，右手高三度。六度相距：右手在主音，左手低六度。每只手的指法仍然跟着音走，不跟着起点走。

Both hands play the same scale from different starting notes. A third apart: left hand on the tonic, right hand a third higher. A sixth apart: right hand on the tonic, left hand a sixth lower. The fingering follows the notes, not the starting point.

半音阶

黑键一律用 3 指，白键用拇指。连续两个白键时多用一个 2 指：右手在 C 和 F，左手在 E 和 B。从哪个音开始都一样。

Every black key takes finger 3 and white keys take the thumb. Where two white keys are adjacent, finger 2 joins in: on C and F in the right hand, on E and B in the left. It works from any starting note.

全音阶

每一步都是全音，一个八度只有 6 个音。总共只有两条：C D E F♯ G♯ A♯ 和 D♭ E♭ F G A B。

Every step is a whole tone, six notes per octave. There are only two of them: C D E F♯ G♯ A♯ and D♭ E♭ F G A B.

属七与减七

属七和弦建在调的第 5 级音上（C 大调是 G B D F），弹完要解决到主音。减七和弦全由小三度叠成，所以总共只有 3 组不同的音。四个音的琶音，白键起音时右手 1-2-3-4，左手 5-4-3-2。

A dominant seventh is built on the 5th degree (G B D F in C major) and resolves to the tonic. A diminished seventh stacks minor thirds, so only three different sets of notes exist. For four-note arpeggios from a white key: right hand 1-2-3-4, left hand 5-4-3-2.

琶音

主音在白键上：右手 1-2-3，左手 5-4-2 或 5-3-2（三音离主音远用 3 指）。主音在黑键上：让拇指落在第一个白键上。

White-key tonic: right hand 1-2-3, left hand 5-4-2 or 5-3-2 (use 3 when the third is a wide reach). Black-key tonic: place the thumb on the first white key.
